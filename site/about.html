About Silk Road

Silk Road helps foreign investors buy property in the historic quarters of Bukhara, Samarkand and Khiva and build hotels for tourists there.

We act as the investor's local partner: we represent your interests, negotiate, hire contractors and run everything on the ground. You do not need to live in Uzbekistan to follow the project. You get reports: how the work is going, what was bought, how much was spent.

We know the local property market and the prices of building materials, and we use that to keep the budget under control. We want a long-term partnership, which is only possible when the investor trusts the numbers in our reports.

From finding a house to the first guests

  1. Selection

    We discuss your budget and hotel format and pick a city. Then we send you suitable properties.

  2. Purchase

    We negotiate, check the documents and register the deal with a notary and the cadastre.

  3. Design and permits

    We commission the design and get the approvals.

  4. Construction

    We hire contractors, buy materials and keep the work and the budget under control.

  5. Opening

    We hand over the finished hotel and help prepare it for operation.
