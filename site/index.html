Hotel property in the old cities of Uzbekistan

We find old houses and plots next to the monuments of Bukhara, Samarkand and Khiva, close the deal and build your hotel turnkey.

View properties

What we do for investors

From finding a house to a turnkey hotel, one team in Uzbekistan handles every step and reports to you on each of them.

Discuss a project

  • Old houses and land plots

    We look for old houses and plots that can become a hotel. For each option you get the location, area and price, and usually several options to compare.

  • Close to the monuments

    We search the historic quarters and the main tourist routes. Travellers choose hotels from which they can walk to the sights.

  • Negotiation and the deal

    We talk to the owners ourselves and aim for a fair market price. Lawyers handle the deal, a notary registers the documents, and we take care of the cadastre.

  • Turnkey hotel

    Permits, design and approvals, builders and craftsmen, materials. We finish when the hotel is ready for its first guests.

  • Quality and deadlines

    We are on site with the contractors. Every stage is accepted on quality and checked against the budget and the schedule.

  • Transparent spending

    We compare suppliers and buy materials where the price is right. You see where the money went and receive reports on the work and purchases.

From finding a house to the first guests

  1. Selection

    We discuss your budget and hotel format and pick a city. Then we send you suitable properties.

  2. Purchase

    We negotiate, check the documents and register the deal with a notary and the cadastre.

  3. Design and permits

    We commission the design and get the approvals.

  4. Construction

    We hire contractors, buy materials and keep the work and the budget under control.

  5. Opening

    We hand over the finished hotel and help prepare it for operation.

Three cities we work in

  • The historic centre of Bukhara is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Many small hotels in the old town operate in former family houses around Lyabi-Hauz and Poi-Kalyan.

  • The Registan, Gur-Emir and Shah-i-Zinda are within walking distance of each other. The high-speed train from Tashkent stops in the city.

  • Itchan Kala, the walled inner city, has been on the UNESCO list since 1990. Residential quarters with old houses lie inside and along the walls.
