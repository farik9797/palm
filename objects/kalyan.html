Landmark

Kalyan Minaret

Built in 1127 under the Karakhanid ruler Arslan Khan. At about 46 metres it can be seen from almost anywhere in the old town. Together with the Kalyan Mosque and the Mir-i-Arab Madrasa it forms the Poi-Kalyan ensemble.
